# BIA on WhatsApp — Overview

## What it is

BIA is Bombino Express's AI assistant. It already lives inside the Bombino app, and now it also answers customers on WhatsApp.

A customer sends a message to the Bombino WhatsApp number and gets a reply within seconds, at any hour. There's nothing to download and no need to log in.

## What customers can ask BIA

| Customers can… | For example |
|---|---|
| **Get a shipping price** | "How much to send 5 kg to New York?" BIA asks for anything missing (destination, weight) and replies with Bombino's live rates. |
| **Track a shipment** | "Where is 925906023?" BIA looks up the tracking number and gives the latest status. Anyone can track any shipment. |
| **Learn how shipping works** | Documents needed, how to pack, what happens after booking, how pickup works. |
| **Start a booking** | When a customer is ready to ship, BIA sends a **Create Shipment** button that opens the Bombino app on the booking screen. |
| **Reach a person** | If the customer asks for a human, or BIA can't help, it shares the Bombino support number (+91 22 6640 0000). |

### A sample conversation

> **Customer:** Hi, what does it cost to send a parcel to the US?
>
> **BIA:** Happy to help. Which city or zip code in the US is it going to, and roughly how much does it weigh?

## What BIA doesn't do yet

- **It can't see a customer's own orders.** On WhatsApp every customer is treated as a guest, so BIA can't list someone's past bookings. If a customer asks, BIA points them to the app. They can still track any shipment by its tracking number.
- **It doesn't make bookings or take payments.** Bookings and payments happen in the app. BIA only sends the customer there.
- **It only replies.** BIA answers when a customer messages first. Order updates that Bombino sends on its own (booking confirmed, pickup on the way and so on) are handled separately.
- **It doesn't hand the chat to a live agent.** For a human, BIA gives the support phone number.

## How it behaves

- **It remembers the conversation.** Within a chat BIA remembers what was said, so customers don't have to repeat themselves. After 30 minutes of silence it starts fresh.
- **No double replies.** Each customer message gets exactly one answer, even if the network hiccups.
- **It never goes silent.** If something goes wrong on our side, the customer is asked to try again in a moment.
- **A fair-use limit.** Each number can send up to 30 messages an hour, enough for a price check and a tracking lookup with room to spare. If someone goes over, BIA politely asks them to continue later or call the team.
- **Privacy.** Customers' full phone numbers are never written into our logs; at most the last four digits appear.

## Usage and billing

We keep a simple monthly report of how much BIA is used, written like an invoice with no technical detail.

- It shows the **number of conversations** each month, with WhatsApp and in-app chats counted separately.
- **On WhatsApp, a conversation is 24 hours from the customer's first message.** Everything they send in that window counts as one conversation. This is the same way our WhatsApp provider, Tata Tele Business Services, bills, so the two numbers line up.
- **In the app, a conversation ends after 30 minutes without a message.**
- Amounts appear on the report once a per-conversation rate is agreed. Until then it shows counts only.
